5. Mass, weight and pressure — keep them separate

Mass

How much matter an object contains. Measured in kg.

Weight

The gravitational force on the object. Measured in N.

Pressure

How force is spread over an area.

pressure = force ÷ area

Everyday model: the same person standing on skis spreads force over a larger area than when standing in narrow shoes, so the pressure on snow is lower.
