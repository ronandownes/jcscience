# JC Science Reference Library

This file defines the reference hierarchy for the JC Science PreTeXt project. Any AI or contributor should consult this alongside the root README before writing substantial content.

## Authority hierarchy

Not all references have equal status.

1. **Curriculum authority** — official Junior Cycle Science specification and Framework for Junior Cycle.
2. **Official Science assessment guidance** — CBAs, Assessment Task, sample questions, learning outcomes and examples of student work.
3. **Professional / administrative guidance** — Inspectorate, school self-evaluation, subject inspection, safety and wider Department/NCCA material.
4. **Assessment corpus** — past and sample examination papers, used to understand assessment demand and to design new original questions.
5. **Commercial reference material** — textbooks and commercial exam papers, used only as working references and never as curricular authority.

If a lower-tier source conflicts with a higher-tier source, follow the higher-tier source.

## Tier 3 — Professional, administrative and safety references

These documents do not define Science content, but should influence the quality of teaching, learning, assessment, inclusion, planning, safety and professional practice built into the resource.

### Looking at Our School 2022 — Post-Primary

Current whole-school quality framework for teaching and learning and leadership and management.

- https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-education/publications/looking-at-our-school-2022/

### A Guide to Inspection in Post-Primary Schools

Use the current version when checking expectations around teaching, learning, planning and assessment.

- https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-education/publications/information-for-teachers-and-school-leaders-post-primary/

### Looking at Junior-Cycle Science

Subject-specific Inspectorate/SSE draft guide. Particularly useful for checking strong science teaching, learning and departmental practice.

- https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-education/publications/sse-resources-post-primary-schools/

Use current SSE material to support evidence-informed review and iterative improvement.

- https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-education/services/school-self-evaluation-sse/
- https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-education/publications/sse-resources-post-primary-schools/

Practical work must be designed with safety as a first-class requirement.

- https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-education/publications/safety-in-school-science/

Historical/administrative laboratory-safety reference, published in 2004.

- https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-education/circulars/aspects-of-safety-in-science-laboratories-in-second-level-schools/

- https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-education/publications/information-for-teachers-and-school-leaders-post-primary/

Older official guidance can be retained where historically useful, but the current version takes precedence.

## Tier 4 — Examination corpus

Past papers are evidence about **assessment**, not the curriculum authority.

Use them to study:

- command/action verbs;
- conceptual demand;
- stimulus material;
- data, graph and table interpretation;
- experimental design and evaluation;
- scientific reasoning;
- common misconceptions;
- response length and structure;
- how knowledge is applied in unfamiliar contexts.

### Target corpus for now

**Current Junior Cycle / common-level era**

- 2019 Science paper
- 2020 archived Science paper, where available
- 2022 Science paper
- 2023 Science paper
- 2024 Science paper
- 2025 Science paper
- 2026 Science paper
- relevant official sample papers, especially 2018/2019 samples

There was no normal 2021 Junior Cycle examination paper in the archive being used.

**Legacy Junior Certificate corpus**

Use Higher and Ordinary Science papers from **2009 through 2018** as an additional question-design corpus.

These legacy papers must not be used to override the current specification. Their value is in the quality and variety of science questions, experimental contexts, diagrams and reasoning tasks that can be abstracted into new current-specification material.

### Paper archive / acquisition index

Maynooth University Mathematics & Statistics Department archive:

- https://archive.maths.nuim.ie/staff/dmalone/StateExamPapers/#JC

Prefer official SEC copies where convenient. The archive is useful as a consolidated discovery/index source.

### Corpus principle

Do not simply reproduce the paper bank inside the book.

The goal is to derive a rich model of **what good science assessment looks like** and then create a much larger original assessment bank aligned explicitly to the current learning outcomes.

## Tier 5 — Commercial reference material

### Textbooks

Two Junior Cycle Science books may be supplied separately as working references.

Use them only to check:

- level;
- coverage;
- explanations;
- examples;
- vocabulary;
- practical work;
- likely omissions.

Do not copy their prose, exercises, illustrations, chapter structure or distinctive presentation.

### Commercial examination papers, including DEB

Commercial papers may be useful in a **private working reference store** for analysing assessment patterns.

Do not treat them as source text for superficial rewriting. Do not publish the original commercial papers in the public repository unless the necessary rights are confirmed.

See `QUESTION-DESIGN.md` for the required method of turning an assessment corpus into genuinely new questions.

---

## Reference-management principle

Keep this list extensive. Contributors should not have to rediscover authoritative documents repeatedly.

For stable official documents, a canonical source link is usually preferable to storing another binary copy in the repository. Store a local copy only when there is a clear workflow reason, and record its source and status.

**Specification first. Official assessment guidance second. Professional guidance informs pedagogy. Past papers inform assessment design. Textbooks and commercial papers are references only.**
